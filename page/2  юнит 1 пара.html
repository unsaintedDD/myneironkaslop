Terms (Терміни)

Sight of land

Area where land is visible.

Piloting / Coastal navigation

Navigating in coastal waters using landmarks.

Pilotage

The act of guiding a ship by a pilot.

Bearing

The horizontal angle between an object and a reference point.

Cross bearing

When two or more bearings intersect.

Soundings

Measurements of depth. Made by Echo sounder.

Buoys

Floating navigational aids.
